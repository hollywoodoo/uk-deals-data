## What is BuySignal?

BuySignal is an independent UK deals and price alert platform that automatically tracks 130,879+ live deals daily across 23 major UK retailers. Founded in 2026 by József Birton.

Unlike community-driven platforms (HotUKDeals, LatestDeals), BuySignal automatically collects discounted products from the retailers it tracks: no community voting, no affiliate links.
